Trust and safety

Work with confidence

Here is how GigsHive helps keep hiring and getting paid clear and fair for both sides.

Verified workers

Workers confirm who they are with an ID check before they can send proposals. Verified profiles show a badge.

Payment held until you approve

When you accept a proposal you fund the contract. The money is held by GigsHive and released to the worker after you approve the work.

A clear record

Every contract shows its status, amount and activity, so both sides can see what was agreed and what happened.

Help when it goes wrong

If there is a problem you can request changes or raise it from the contract. Our team can look into it.

How a payment moves

You accept a proposal
A contract is created
You fund it
Money is held by GigsHive
You approve the work
Payment goes to the worker

Stay safe on GigsHive

  • ✓Keep the conversation and payment on GigsHive so your contract is recorded.
  • ✓Never share your password or a code sent to you, not even with someone who says they work for us.
  • ✓Check that a worker shows the verified badge before you hire.
  • ✓Only approve work when you are happy with it. Approving releases the payment.
  • ✓If something feels wrong, report it from the contract page.
