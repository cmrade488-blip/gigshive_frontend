How your payment is protected

The money moves in clear steps, and every step is recorded on your wallet.

1. Funded

The client pays into the contract. The worker sees that the contract is funded before starting.

2. Held

The payment stays held while the work is done and delivered for review.

3. Released

When the client approves, or the review window ends, the payment is released to the worker.

4. Withdrawn

The worker withdraws to a Nigerian bank account. Fees are shown on the contract before you pay.
