About us

Hire. Work. Get paid.

GigsHive is a marketplace where good work meets fair payment.

Why GigsHive exists

Hiring someone you have not met is a risk, and so is working before you are paid. GigsHive puts both on the same side: payment is held until the work is approved, and people confirm who they are.

One account lets you hire when you need help and offer your skills when you have time.

What we believe

Fair for both sides

Clients know what they pay. Workers know they will be paid for approved work.

Clear and honest

We show amounts, fees and status plainly. No hidden steps.

Skills over connections

Good work should be found by the quality of the work, wherever the worker is.
